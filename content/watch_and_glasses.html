if Apple watch and Google glasses work together






Today Apple watch has started selling, but Google glasses seems to have been forgotten. Google glasses has once attracted a lot of attention, but it has no killer application up to now. Let's think about if Apple watch and Google glasses work together, what will happen.


One of the big disputed point of Google glasses is it's camera, so taking away the camera from Google glasses is a wise choice. it can avoid privacy disputes, and can also reduce power consumption and reduce the need of processing power.


Let the Apple watch and Google glasses synchronize their screen display.when there’s nothing to show, Google glasses is like a normal glasses . When you want to see the time, lift your wrist, the time is displayed in front of your eyes. For other type of message,it will display even don’t need to lift wrist. Only when you need to operate you have to lift your wrist. Voice messages can also be played through Google glass headphones.


If you see a man wearing glasses who are raising his head , looking at nothing and giggling. his hand is touching the his watch (in operation), You will not feel strange.


Of course, these scenarios are imaged, Only if Apple sells Apple glasses or Google sells Google watch,it can be seen realistically.
