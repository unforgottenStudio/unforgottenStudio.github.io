Sora 2's Most Ingenious Design: Turning Millions of Users into "Invisible" AI Trainers



The user interaction model of the Sora 2 app reveals a clever design: every time a user publishes or discards a video, it acts as a "vote of taste" for the AI, directly shaping the model's aesthetics and capabilities.

This is essentially a form of large-scale, decentralized Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Each user choice helps define what a "good" video is for Sora 2, enabling its continuous iteration and optimization. This is reminiscent of Midjourney's classic "pick one of four" preference selection, which also cleverly converts user behavior into a high-quality data stream for model training.

This model blurs the line between "user" and "co-creator." We are not just consumers of content; we are also invisibly participating in the AI's evolution. This might signal the core logic for future AIGC product iteration: the real competitive moat may not be the superiority of the algorithm, but rather the construction of a self-evolving ecosystem—a "data flywheel" driven by collective user intelligence.
